# Code of Conduct

We follow the [Contributor Covenant 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

In short: be kind, assume good faith, and keep discussions about the work. Harassment,
insults and sharing anyone's personal or financial data are not tolerated.

Report problems privately to the maintainers through GitHub's private reporting. Maintainers
will review every report and may warn, suspend or ban contributors who break these rules.
